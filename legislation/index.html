01 Print-blocking laws

Two states now require blocking technology on 3D printers.

Enacted · Ch. 461, Stats. 2026

California AB 2047

If ASTM publishes standards showing blocking is feasible, California DOJ sets minimum performance standards. A year later, 3D printers sold in the state must include firearm blocking technology.

Read the explainer Enacted · Ch. 55, Laws of 2026

New York’s 3D printer law

A state working group decides whether blocking is technologically feasible. If it is, 3D printers sold in New York must include it one year after rules are issued.
