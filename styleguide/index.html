02

Quick guidance

Simple constraints keep the identity recognizable.

01

Give it room

Keep clear space around the artwork equal to at least half the cube’s width.

02

Preserve proportion

Scale uniformly. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or rearrange the lockup.

03

Maintain contrast

Use ink on light backgrounds and the white or coral-accent versions on black.
